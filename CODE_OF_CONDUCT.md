# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want everyone who participates in this project — users, contributors,
reviewers and maintainers — to have a respectful experience regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, level of experience, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient, and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language; many participants are not native
  English speakers.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments about any person or group.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing others' private information (addresses, contact details, login
  credentials) without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces: the issue tracker, pull
requests, discussions, and any public channel where someone is representing
the project. It also applies when someone is acting on the project's behalf
outside those spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer
([@Perchemop](https://github.com/Perchemop)) via GitHub, or to the email address
listed on their GitHub profile. Security reports follow a separate private path
(see `SECURITY.md`). All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and
fairly.

Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's identity is not shared
with the person reported. Confidentiality extends to any details shared
beyond the need to resolve the report.

## Enforcement

1. **Private feedback.** For a first minor incident, the maintainer gives a
   written private explanation of why the behaviour was inappropriate.
2. **Public warning.** A clear warning with consequences for continued
   behaviour.
3. **Temporary ban.** Suspension from participation for a defined period.
4. **Permanent ban.** Sustained or severe violations.

Decisions escalate based on severity and repetition, not on seniority. A
person who has been warned, or whose report was upheld, and who repeats the
behaviour may move straight to the highest applicable step.
